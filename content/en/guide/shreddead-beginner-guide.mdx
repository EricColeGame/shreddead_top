## What kind of game is ShredDead?

ShredDead is a short incremental zombie-shredding game by Weird Penguin Games. You face endless waves, with options to attack zombies yourself or have grunts help. The developer describes it as a relaxing game with **no lose conditions**, so you can focus on trying its progression systems rather than protecting a perfect run.

## A simple way to get started

- **Fight the incoming zombies.** You can take part directly, use your grunts, or combine both approaches.
- **Pay attention to loot.** Zombies deliver loot that you can use to equip your grunts.
- **Explore the skill tree.** It offers ways to improve how you deal with zombies, though the official description does not identify a best first choice.
- **Try the shredder.** The industrial shredder is one of the game’s featured ways to dispose of zombies.

Treat these as systems to explore, not a mandatory sequence. The available official information does not establish which choice is fastest.

## How progression expands

As you continue defeating zombies, ShredDead gives you more to consider than direct attacks. The skill tree supports different ways to optimize your setup, while loot lets you improve your grunts’ equipment. A mad scientist also lets you evolve the zombies.
