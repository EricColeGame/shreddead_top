## ShredDead on Steam: The quick answer

Planning to play ShredDead? Its Steam platform listing matters before you buy or download it: the game supports **Windows and Mac**, and its listed release date is **October 26, 2026**. Linux is not listed as supported in the [official ShredDead Steam app details](https://store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=4704520&l=english).

## Which platforms support ShredDead?

Steam’s platform data marks Windows and Mac as supported. It marks Linux as unsupported. That tells you which desktop operating systems the listing supports as of October 29, 2026; it does not establish whether support for other platforms might be added later.

## When was ShredDead released?

Steam lists **October 26, 2026** as ShredDead’s release date and does not mark the game as “coming soon.” Weird Penguin Games is listed as its developer.

## What kind of game is it?

ShredDead is an incremental zombie-shredding game built around progression without a lose condition. Its Steam description highlights endless zombie waves, loot collection, a skill tree, and evolving zombies. Those details give you a sense of the game, but the platform listing does not provide device-specific performance information.
